## Context

Database engineering project focused on modeling and deploying a reliable **concert ticketing system** on **Microsoft SQL Server**.

## Modeling & Implementation

- **Relational schema**: normalized Entity-Relationship design covering artists, venues, seating categories, orders, and tickets.
- **Data integrity**: primary/foreign keys, `CHECK` constraints (capacity, dates, pricing), and targeted indexes.
- **T-SQL analytics**: complex joins and aggregations tracking venue occupancy, top-selling events, and revenue by period.

## Key Takeaway

Built a durable foundation in **relational modeling** and **SQL Server querying**, directly applicable to production data engineering.
