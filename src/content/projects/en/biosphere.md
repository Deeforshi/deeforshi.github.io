## Context

**Biosphere** is a **Java** implementation of a turn-based board game centered on **2D array** algorithms and deterministic rule enforcement.

## Architecture & Algorithms

- **Grid modeling**: matrix representation of tiles, resources, and adjacent neighbor lookups.
- **Rule engine**: legal move generation, tile interaction resolution, and live score calculation.
- **Automated validation**: unit tests covering placement rules, edge coordinates, and end-game states.

## Outcome

Strengthened algorithmic fluency in **Java** and experience building deterministic, fully testable game engines.
