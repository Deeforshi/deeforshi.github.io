## Context

Conducted in a **5-person team**, this study examines the economic, technological, and competitive positioning of **Capgemini**, a global leader in IT services and digital transformation (ESN).

## Methodology & Deliverable

- **PESTEL Framework**: evaluated Political, Economic, Sociocultural, Technological (Cloud, Data, AI), Environmental (Green IT), and Legal drivers.
- **Porter’s Five Forces**: analyzed client and talent bargaining power, industry rivalry, and barriers to entry across IT consulting.
- **Published Report**: synthesized team research into a structured PDF report designed for both technical and management audiences.

## Key Takeaway

Deepened understanding of **IT services market dynamics** and data-backed strategic analysis.
